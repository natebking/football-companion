# Project review, 2026-09-05 (evening)

Claude's review of the project at `c849c7d` and the live site at https://fluentin.football, requested by Nathan after Codex's build day. Four questions: are the methods sound, does the product make sense, what would add value, what to do about search visibility and analytics.

Inputs: the repo and its six 2026-09-05 docs; a replay of the live path over six real ESPN games (1,036 poll states); the raw HTML of the live site; a seven-lens Claude review (methodology, pedagogy, code, live path, product, GEO, analytics) whose findings are marked **[lens]**; an independent Codex review marked **[Codex]**; and Claude's own reading of the code, marked **[verified]** where Claude confirmed a claim at the cited line. The lens skeptic pass was cut short by an account limit, so lens findings without **[verified]** are one reviewer's claim with evidence, not a confirmed defect.

## Verdict

The engine and the truth boundary are ahead of the product. The tendency method is honest, its numbers reproduce from the parquets, the live-feed repair is measured, and nothing in PRIME can see an outcome before the delay. The product shell undoes that care in three places: a first-run that auto-selects a live game with a zero delay and shows its raw score, a Games sheet that prints undelayed scores, and a "Where to look" line that on nine snaps in ten has nothing to do with the tendency printed under it or the call demanded. The learning design has commitment but no cue and no reveal of the cue, so what it trains today is reading a percentage. The next month of value is score-and-clock context in the tables, a cue-bound ask, and a pre-rendered `/learn/` section that finally puts the project's citable numbers where a crawler can read them. None of it should be shared publicly until the two spoiler paths, the ESPN terms question, and one line in prior-art.md are dealt with.

## 1. Methodology

**Sound.** Chronological holdout, Brier against a situation-matched league baseline, a team-clustered paired bootstrap, shrinkage fitted on a nested split that never touches the holdout. The lens re-derived every headline number from the parquets and got the same values (NFL 2025 0.21896 vs 0.21901, CFB 0.22740 vs 0.22880, same intervals) **[lens]**. Sample floor of 30 and the rung-2/3 widening measure as immaterial (under 0.0003 Brier) **[lens]**. The live-feed repair rules are conservative and each one was measured on real data (CONTRACT.md "Live feed repair") **[verified]**.

**Overclaimed or mislabeled.**

- **Recency weights the rates but shrinkage trusts the raw count** **[verified, Codex]**. `engine/tendency.py` ~150 to 160 weights sums by season (1.0, 0.5, 0.25) but `sample_size` stays raw, and the code comment argues the reverse of what the math does: raw counts let three-year-old plays buy full trust. Brier is unaffected because `k` was fitted under the same convention; the `high` and `medium` confidence labels are not what they claim. Fix: an effective weighted `n`, then refit `k`.
- **Team attribution is real but about half the size printed** **[lens, unverified]**. Regressing realized deviation on printed deviation over attributable snaps gives a slope near 0.5 in the static NFL test and 0.7 in CFB, so "They throw here 78%" over a 68% league line realizes near 73%. The audit never measures this because it scores the engine block, not the printed number.
- **The NFL "statistical tie" tests week-1 conditions, not what the cron ships** **[lens, unverified]**. A rolling forward-chain that rebuilds each week gives a difference twelve times the static point estimate, still inside the interval. Quote the rolling protocol beside the static one, and keep NFL team cards. Note that DEPLOYMENT.md says the Vercel project has no Git integration, so a Tuesday table refresh reaches the phone only if someone deploys **[Codex, lens]**.
- **Sacks are pass plays in the tables and voided picks on the phone** **[lens; the two definitions confirmed in `engine/ingest_nfl.py` and `web/play-facts.js`]**. The printed pass rate runs about 1.8 points above the share of plays a viewer is graded on. Pick one definition; the cheapest is to grade a sack as a pass.
- "Call the play" is stronger than the data supports; the target is the recorded action **[Codex]**.

**The one change that most improves the number a card prints:** add score band and clock phase to the league baseline. The completed pilot on 2018 to 2025 NFL data moved held-out Brier from 0.21960 to 0.20298 and run/pass accuracy from 62% to 67% (`docs/COACHING-PILOT-2026-09-05.md`), roughly two hundred times the team effect; the lens's 2025 re-derivation with a coarse 3-by-4 grid lands in the same place **[Codex doc; lens unverified]**. The client already has period, clock and score on the pre-snap whitelist, and the three late-game cards go numberless today because the bucket average is wrong exactly where the tendency is most lopsided (late and trailing, offenses pass near 80% against a 57% bucket average) **[lens]**. Ship a `league_context` table keyed `bucket|score_band|phase`, use it as the baseline, keep the team delta on the bucket, and gate it on a calibration table in the audit.

## 2. Does the product make sense as built

Yes at the level of the brief. Not yet at the level of a stranger with a phone. Ranked:

1. **The eye target is decoupled from the tendency and the call** **[verified at `web/app.js` 652 and 675; lens, Codex]**. `st.lesson` is chosen by an arithmetic hash of down, distance, spot and period; the watch line takes the lesson over the card's authored watch whenever a lesson exists, which is every 1st to 3rd down. So on about 90% of snaps the card prints "They throw here 54%" above "If a pass is caught, note where the catch happens", a line about a post-snap event on a card labeled "Before the snap". The 22 authored cue lines render only on 4th down. Prior-art section 4 says the effect in Muller 2024 comes from committing on the occluded cue; here the viewer can only call from the percentage. Fix: one system, not two. When an ask fires, the watch line comes from the card (cue and call share a target); the lesson stays behind "See the idea". Or give each card a fixed lesson id so the pairing is authored, not hashed.
2. **The forced call sits below the fold on a phone** **[lens, Codex]**. Journal toolbar, field diagram and "See the idea" push the buttons past 800 px on a 375-wide screen. Cut in this order: journal toolbar off the live page, field diagram hidden by default, chips behind one disclosure, latest play only.
3. **Wrong default level for the named audience** **[Codex, lens]**. "Read the game" is the default; "Start with basics" is under the schedule and appearance controls. Default to basics on a new device.
4. **The ask budget is spent on coin flips** **[lens]**. `askEligible` never looks at the printed rate; in the six-game replay 35 of 59 numbered pass/run asks fired on rates between 42% and 58%. One line: skip the ask when `|rate - 0.5| < 0.08`. Related: the first card asks immediately because `sinceAsk` starts at 99 **[verified]**; for a beginner make the first three cards observation-only.
5. **The Games sheet is a settings drawer and How-it-works is 300 words** **[Codex, lens]**. Split Games from Settings, move diagnostics behind an advanced disclosure, cut How-it-works to three points and promote the delay sheet into onboarding.

What to cut: "So far in this game" (the stat dashboard the brief rejects, per the lens), the journal toolbar on the live page, and the review's maintainer-facing copy.

## 5. Analytics

### Today

No tracking of any kind **[verified]**: eleven first-party scripts, nothing else; `/_vercel/insights/script.js` is 404; the Vercel API reports `web_analytics_not_enabled`; the team is on Pro. The site promises "Your journal stays in this browser" and the diagnostics button sends nothing until the user copies it. Both are true and should stay true.

### Tier 1, this week: Vercel Web Analytics

First-party path, 3 KB script, no cookies, no localStorage, visitor hash discarded after 24 hours **[lens read the script and Vercel's 2026-06-26 privacy page]**. It does store city-level location, referrer, OS and browser, so the site's copy must say so. Pro pricing is per event: about $0.03 per 1,000, so a thousand game-viewers a month is roughly $1.50 **[lens; corrects an earlier draft of this section]**.

1. Enable: `vercel project web-analytics football-companion --scope nates-projects-925609f4`, or Project, Analytics, Enable in the dashboard.
2. In `web/index.html` before `play-facts.js`:

```html
<script>
window.va = window.va || function () { (window.vaq = window.vaq || []).push(arguments); };
window.va('beforeSend', function (e) {
  try { if (localStorage.getItem('fc_no_analytics') === '1') return null; } catch (x) {}
  return e;
});
</script>
<script defer src="/_vercel/insights/script.js"></script>
```

3. A "Count my visits" checkbox in Settings that writes `fc_no_analytics`, so no banner is needed and the opt-out is visible.
4. Replace the privacy sentence in How-it-works with: "Fluent in Football counts visits with Vercel Web Analytics. It sets no cookies and keeps no name or account; it records the page, your browser and operating system, the site that sent you, and your city. Your journal and practice log never leave this browser unless you download or copy them. Untick Count my visits in Settings to opt out."

### Tier 2, next: product events

Route events through the existing `diag()` sink in SHELL with a whitelist, so PRIME and LIVE are untouched and nothing crosses the delay queue (grade events fire only in the `result` handler, after release) **[lens]**. Pro allows two properties per custom event, so fold kind into the value:

| event | where | value |
|---|---|---|
| `game_picked` | LIVE `selectGame` | league |
| `delay_set` | SHELL `setDelay`, debounced | bucket: 0, 1 to 30, 31 to 60, 61 to 90 |
| `ask_answered` | PRIME `askBtns` click | `passrun_2to4s` |

| `halftime`, `diagnostics_copied`, `journal_opened`, `review_opened`, `lesson_opened` | their buttons | lesson id |

About 45 events per game-viewer. Do one more thing in the same commit because rows cannot be backfilled: add `rate` and `attributed` to the ask log row (`st.pending` and `logAskRow`) and raise `ASK_LOG_MAX` from 400 to 1,500. Without the printed rate on the row, nobody can tell a correct call that followed the number from one that read the field, which is the product's whole thesis **[lens; the omission is verified in the code]**.

Add an explicit "Share my practice log" action that uploads the diagnostics JSON, with the consent on the button: "Sends your practice log (calls, grades, delay settings) to Fluent in Football to improve the lessons.
