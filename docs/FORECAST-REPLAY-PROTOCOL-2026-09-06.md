# Forecast replay and prospective freeze

This closes the probability-replay part of the advanced-mode plan. It does not replace the live estimator. The existing 2026 journals are a development sample: their outcomes have already been inspected. No result from them may be described as prospective performance.

Freeze separate NFL and college candidates for the 2026 season before examining later sessions. Refit the existing context/team estimator through 2025 using the context and identity shrinkage already selected by the fixed 2024 procedure. Retain the existing sigmoid calibration, including for college; do not choose its uncalibrated alternative on the basis of the 2025 test results. Use the existing source-clock and action-label repairs. No 2026 outcome is a training or calibration input. Save both candidates, the currently shipped baseline tables, input/source-code hashes and a UTC freeze time together. Never overwrite a freeze directory.

For each exported journal, use only its saved pre-snap situation as the prediction input. Resolve the offense from the saved team identity using the app's existing resolver. Missing score, clock or required context stays missing. Do not fill it from the final report. Record both the frozen baseline's attributable/league estimate and the candidate's calibrated estimate. A saved displayed percentage is a third, distinct value: it may have been rounded or generated by an older build.

Link an input to an outcome only with its explicit saved target/resolution play ID. Do not infer the next play from game-clock equality or matching down and distance. The input must have been recorded visible, after its basis play was released and strictly before the target's first recorded arrival. Reject conflicting target links, missing basis evidence, missing arrivals and duplicate scoring of a target play. Retain the latest qualifying visible input for that target; keep the other rows with exclusion reasons. A hidden percentage can support an offline counterfactual, but is never counted as a displayed forecast.

Outcomes require a separate source-alignment artifact with exact game, play and offense identity, final source provenance and the same pass/rush definitions as training. Quiz grades and voids are not labels: sacks belong to recorded passes and scrambles to rushes, while no-plays, kneels and kicking plays are excluded. Check the stored situation against the reported target's down, distance, field position and offense, but never substitute those later values into a prediction. Later clock/score differences are diagnostics, not repaired inputs.

Report coverage and exclusions before errors. Existing journals go in the development section. Prospective eligibility requires saved season 2026, a regular/postseason type, a shown time after the freeze, and a source-aligned outcome. This is still a selective sample of visible saved moments, not every play or every user. Do not merge NFL and college. Prespecified minimum for an aggregate prospective readout is 2,000 eligible plays across 20 games per league; until then, show counts and individual diagnostics without claiming an accuracy improvement. Once available, report paired Brier error, game-clustered uncertainty and calibration bins alongside missingness and timing issues. This minimum enables a readout; it does not itself authorize model replacement.

Useful/obvious/unsupported feedback and historical practice answers remain separate outcomes. Neither a better forecast error nor matching a final feed proves that viewers learned more. No automatic retraining or background collection is introduced.
