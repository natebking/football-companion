How it works

Pick a game, then keep watching on TV. Before the snap, a hint suggests where to look. Tap an underlined football term for a definition.

The percentages come from past run and pass choices in similar situations. They do not tell us the next play or how anyone is lined up. The app does not watch your TV picture.

Recent plays explains what happened and what changed. Player names, formations, and direction appear when the feed reports them. Updates can arrive late or be corrected.

If the app gets ahead of your TV, use TV delay to hold cards, plays, and scores by 0–90 seconds. Adjust it as you watch. Your occasional picks are practice: a correct prediction does not measure your understanding of a football concept.
